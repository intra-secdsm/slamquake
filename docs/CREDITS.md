promotions in it are fictional. It's released under the MIT License ([`LICENSE`](../LICENSE)).
Third-party software and its licenses are listed in full in
[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). The game shows the same information under
**Options → Credits & licenses**.

## Audio policy
Every audio file must be listed in `game/assets/audio/licenses.json` with its source, author and license.
Only CC0 or similarly permissive audio may be added. So far there's no third-party audio: everything is
original and generated by the scripts above. Every melody, riff and drum pattern is written in
`tools/synth_music.py`.
