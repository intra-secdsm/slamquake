## Audio
Every audio file is listed with source URL, author, and license in
`game/assets/audio/licenses.json`. Only CC0 or equivalently permissive audio is accepted.

All audio so far is original and generated by our own scripts (CC0); no third-party audio is used yet:
- Sound effects (impacts, bell, pyro, crowd reactions) in `game/assets/audio/sfx/`: `tools/synth_sfx.py`.
- Music (menu theme, wrestler entrance themes, victory fanfare) in `game/assets/audio/music/` and the crowd
  ambience loops: `tools/synth_music.sh` (numpy, run with Blender's bundled Python). Every melody, riff and
  drum pattern is written in that script.
